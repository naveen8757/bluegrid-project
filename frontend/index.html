The Island Energy Dilemma

Remote archipelagos like Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar depend almost entirely on imported diesel barged over rough seas, resulting in extreme electricity costs ($0.35–$0.60/kWh), high carbon footprints, and fragile supply chains.

Why OTEC + Marine Hybrid?

While solar and wind are intermittent, Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC) utilizes the steep ~22°C temperature gradient between tropical surface water and 1,000m deep cold ocean currents to generate constant 24/7 clean baseload power alongside freshwater desalination.

Autonomous Optimization

BlueGrid AI automates the complex multi-variable microgrid planning problem: matching diurnal load curves, sizing modular solar PV, coastal wind, OTEC, and battery storage to maximize fuel savings without risking blackouts.
